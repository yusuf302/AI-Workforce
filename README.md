## Project Overview

This project uses the **July 2026 Onyx Data DataDNA workforce dataset and challenge brief** as a practical case study.

The analysis covers **2021–2024** and examines how AI adoption relates to:

- job displacement
- job creation
- reskilling investment
- industry exposure
- skill transition

The dashboard is structured as a five-part analytical story:

**Executive Overview → Country Intelligence → Industry Exposure → Skills Transition → Reskilling & Policy**

---

## Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average AI Adoption | **30.65%** |
| Jobs Displaced | **586,785** |
| Jobs Created | **296,355** |
| Net Employment Gap | **-290,430** |
| Reskilling Investment | **$61.65M** |
| Average Risk Index | **4.26 / 10** |

A key pattern in the analysis is the acceleration in AI adoption after **2022-Q4**, while job creation continued to cover only about half of total displacement.

## Dashboard Sections

### 01 — Executive Overview
Tracks AI adoption, job displacement, job creation, net employment, reskilling spend, and overall risk over time.

### 02 — Country Intelligence
Compares countries and regions by displacement, reskilling investment, creation coverage, adoption, and risk.

### 03 — Industry Exposure
Identifies industries facing the largest employment gaps and highlights where reskilling support is weak relative to workforce exposure.

### 04 — Skills Transition
Examines which skill categories face the widest transition gaps and where AI adoption may create both augmentation and displacement pressure.

### 05 — Reskilling & Policy
Brings the findings together into a prioritization view focused on displacement volume, risk, and reskilling intensity.
