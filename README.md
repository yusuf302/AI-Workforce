## 📊 Key Highlights

* **Adoption vs. Net Employment:** Analyzes post-2022 Q4 Generative AI acceleration, job creation coverage, and global net employment gaps.
* **Country & Industry Risk:** Evaluates displacement metrics and reskilling spend per displaced job across key regions and sectors.
* **Skill Category Pathways:** Identifies transition risks across manual, clerical, technical, and managerial skill groups.
