Excited to share my latest Power BI dashboard project: **AI Workforce Transformation Index**.

The dashboard explores how AI adoption is reshaping jobs, displacement risk, and reskilling priorities across countries, industries, and skill categories.

Key message: AI adoption accelerated sharply after the generative-AI era began in 2022-Q4, but job creation is not keeping pace with displacement. That means reskilling investment needs to be targeted by exposure and employment gap, not adoption alone.

Highlights:
- 30.65% average AI adoption rate
- 586.8K jobs displaced vs 296.4K jobs created
- 50.5% job creation coverage
- -290.4K net employment gap
- Priority sectors include Food & Beverage, Retail & E-commerce, Media & Entertainment, Automotive, and Manufacturing

Built with Power BI using a policy-briefing design approach: clear message, no map visuals, and decision-ready recommendations.
